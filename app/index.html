    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx; Push Square says 250+). One playthrough — there is no mission replay and no New Game+. Cheats disable trophies and stop the game saving, so keep them off your platinum save. The game has 15 manual save slots and 1 autosave: keep a save at the start and end of each chapter.
